Use the appropriate issue template:
- **bug report** — something is broken
- **feature request** — something is missing
- **component request** — feed the v0.2+ roadmap

## Commercial support

Pro tier customers (Indie $99/yr, Team $249/yr) get:
- 48-hour response on issues tagged `pro`
- A private Discord channel
- Quarterly office hours

Enterprise customers get:
- Custom SLA
- Dedicated support channel
- On-prem license server deployment
