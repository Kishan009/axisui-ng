## How to add yourself

When your first PR is merged, the maintainer will add you to this file. The format is:

```
- **Your Name** (@github-handle) — [first contribution date] — [what you contributed]
```

Recognition: a monthly "top contributor" callout appears in the changelog. Substantial contributors may be invited to become maintainers.
